How to Fight!👊🏼

Attack

Regular attacks deal standard damage to your enemy. These are your basic moves and can be used frequently in combat.

Remember! These attacks do not use any energy, but they also don't cause as much damage!

Special Move

Special attacks unleash powerful moves that deal heavy damage or provide buffs. Use them strategically to turn the tide of battle in your favor.

Remember! Special moves cost energy! So make sure to keep an eye on your hero's health before attacking!
